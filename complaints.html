Accountability applies to us too

A fair process must be open to criticism.

Students and supporters should have a safe way to raise concerns about errors, unfairness, privacy, or how a case was handled.

What the future process should include

  1. A confidential route to raise a complaint.
  2. Review by someone not involved in the original decision, where possible.
  3. A written explanation of the outcome and any corrective action.
  4. A way to report inaccurate information and request correction.
  5. Protection against retaliation for good-faith complaints.

What is not active yet

This demonstration website does not have a staffed complaint channel. A real contact route and responsible reviewer must be appointed before the pilot opens.

Our standard

We will not treat criticism as disloyalty. A complaint can reveal a mistake, a gap in policy, or a conflict of interest—and should be assessed on its merits.

If there is an immediate safety concern, use an appropriate trusted adult or relevant local service rather than relying on a future S.H.I.E.L.D. process.
