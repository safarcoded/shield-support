What we stand for

Support should start with listening.

A penalty can be disputed for different reasons. We want students to have a respectful way to explain what happened and understand the options available to them.

Fair review

Look at the student's account, available evidence, relevant rules, and uncertainties before drawing conclusions.

Privacy by default

Keep identities and case details private. Public storytelling is never a condition for seeking help.

Practical support

Offer clear guidance and consider appropriate assistance only when eligibility, resources, and safeguards permit.

The process

Clear steps. Respect at every stage.

The pilot process is designed to be understandable and accountable—not a black box.

01

Tell us

02

Screen

03
