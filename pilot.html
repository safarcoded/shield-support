What needs to happen before the pilot opens?

01

Confirm the pilot scope

Select the city, participating institutes, and cases the team can responsibly handle.

02

Establish governance

Identify reviewers, adult oversight, financial controls, and complaint handlers.

03

Secure the information flow

Test access controls, privacy notices, retention rules, and deletion procedures.

04

Test before opening

Run fictional cases, test accessibility, and publish realistic response targets.
