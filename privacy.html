Privacy by default

People are not proof of a cause.

A student's identity, hardship, and case details deserve protection. Privacy is a design requirement, not an optional extra.

$

Draft policy. This page describes intended principles. A final privacy notice must identify the actual operator, systems, retention periods, contact route, and applicable legal requirements before real personal information is collected.

Our intended principles

Collect only what is needed

Initial inquiries should request the minimum information necessary. Sensitive evidence should be requested only when it is relevant to a case.

Restrict access

Case information should be accessible only to authorized people who need it for their assigned role. Reviewers should follow confidentiality and conflict-of-interest rules.

No public disclosure by default

Names, photographs, documents, and personal stories should not be published by default. A third party's request does not authorize disclosure. Any exceptional sharing requires specific, informed permission and appropriate safeguards.

Extra care for minors

Because many students are under 18, the operational process must include suitable safeguarding and guardian involvement where appropriate. A guardian's involvement should not be used to expose a student unnecessarily.

Retention and deletion

Before accepting real cases, the team must establish how long each category of information is kept, who can delete it, and how deletion requests are handled.

Security limitations

This static website does not provide secure case storage. Do not submit real case details, identity documents, payment information, or sensitive personal information through any demonstration form.
