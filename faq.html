Questions, answered plainly

What you should know before seeking support.

Honest answers are part of building trust.

Is S.H.I.E.L.D. accepting cases right now?

No. This is a public informational preview while the supervised pilot and its safeguards are being prepared.

Can S.H.I.E.L.D. pay my fine?

Financial assistance is not currently available through this website. Any future aid will depend on eligibility, available funds, verification, and appropriate oversight.

Does S.H.I.E.L.D. assume every student is innocent?

No. The intended process considers evidence and context. A student may have broken a rule while still having a reasonable concern about whether a penalty was proportionate.

Does an institution's refusal to respond prove wrongdoing?

No. The team should explain what information was available and what could not be independently confirmed.

Will donors see my name or photograph?

Not by default. A contribution or request for information does not authorize access to a student's identity. Any exceptional sharing requires specific, informed permission and appropriate safeguards.

Can S.H.I.E.L.D. guarantee an appeal will succeed?

No. It cannot control an institution's decision or guarantee an outcome.

Can I submit documents through this website?

No. This static preview is not a secure case-management system. Do not enter or send sensitive personal or case information through demonstration features.

Where will the pilot begin?

The intended first pilot will work with a small group of institutes in one city. The city and participating institutions have not yet been finalized publicly.
