A fair process

Clear steps. No false promises.

The proposed process is designed to be understandable, evidence-based, and respectful. It will be tested during the supervised pilot before service targets are published.

01

Initial inquiry

The student explains the concern through an approved intake channel. Only essential information should be collected initially.

02

Scope and eligibility check

The team checks whether the matter fits the pilot's defined scope and explains any limitations.

03

Evidence review

Ordinary cases are intended to receive two independent reviews. Complex or disputed cases may go to a panel.

04

Reasoned outcome

The student receives an explanation of the decision, what could be established, and what remains uncertain.

05

Guidance and possible assistance

Guidance may be offered within the pilot's capacity. Financial aid is a separate decision and is not guaranteed.

06

Correction or second review

The proposed policy provides a route to correct inaccurate information, raise a complaint, or request a second review.

$

Important: S.H.I.E.L.D. is not a court, regulator, or substitute for professional legal advice. It cannot guarantee that an institution will change its decision.

Examples of potentially relevant evidence

  • A written penalty or notice
  • The student's account of events
  • Relevant institutional rules
  • Witness accounts, if safely available
  • An institutional response, when reasonably obtainable

Do not send sensitive evidence to this demonstration website.
