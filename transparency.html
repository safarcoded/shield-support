What we intend to publish

  • Current policies and version dates
  • Aggregate case statistics when available
  • Financial summaries and accounting reports when applicable
  • How review decisions and complaints are handled

What we will protect

  • Student identities and contact details
  • Private case evidence
  • Financial hardship details
  • Any information that could identify a student indirectly
Funding safeguards

No contributions until the controls are ready.

Before accepting public contributions or arranging assistance, the initiative needs appropriate adult oversight, transparent accounting, a suitable payment process, and relevant legal and safeguarding checks.
