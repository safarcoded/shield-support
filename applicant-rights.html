Respect at every step

Seeking help should never cost you your dignity.

These proposed rights will form part of the pilot's operating policy and should be finalized before applications open.

Respectful treatment

Applicants should be treated with respect regardless of financial circumstances, social status, or the outcome of their case.

Privacy

Applicants should know who can access their information and how it will be used.

Reasons for decisions

Applicants should receive an understandable explanation of decisions and any important uncertainties.

Correction and second review

Applicants should have a way to correct inaccurate information and request review under the published process.

Withdraw an inquiry

Applicants should be able to withdraw, subject to any necessary records or obligations explained in the final policy.

No pressure to publicize

Applicants should not need to share photographs, stories, or public endorsements to be considered.
